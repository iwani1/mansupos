Privacy notice

Pilot version · last updated 23 November 2026

Draft for the pilot. This notice is written to follow Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) but has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer, and Mansu POS's registration with the Data Protection Commission must be completed before general launch.

Who is responsible

For your account and billing, Mansu POS is the data controller. For the data your shop records about its own customers and staff, your shop is the controller and Mansu POS processes it on your behalf, only to run the service.

What we collect

  • Account: shop name, shop address, store address, owner's name, email, phone, usernames. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Shop records: sales, receipts, shifts, stock changes, returns, staff names and roles, and any customer names or phone numbers your staff enter.
  • Store connection: the WooCommerce keys you approve, stored encrypted and used only to talk to your store.
  • Billing: plan and payment records. Card and mobile money details are handled by Paystack; we never see or store them.
  • Technical: sign-in times, IP addresses and error logs, kept to protect the service.
  • Feedback you send us from your shop.

Why we use it

  • To run your shop's till and keep your store's stock in step (performing our contract with you).
  • To bill you and send service emails such as sign-up confirmation, trial and payment reminders.
  • To keep the service secure, investigate problems and improve it.

We do not sell personal data and do not use your shop's data for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Paystack (payments), Resend (sending email) and our server host. They act on our instructions. Some of them may store data outside Ghana; we use providers that protect it adequately.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your shop is open, and then for up to 90 days after it closes so you can come back or export, unless you ask us to delete sooner. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires. Daily backups are kept for 14 days.

Security

Each shop's data is kept in its own database. Connections are encrypted, store keys are encrypted at rest, staff sign-ins are rate-limited and console access needs a second factor.

Your rights

Under Act 843 you may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Your shop's customers and staff can ask your shop, and we will help you answer. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Contact

Email the address that sends your Mansu POS emails, or use the Feedback page in your shop.
