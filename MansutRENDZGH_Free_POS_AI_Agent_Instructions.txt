PROJECT BRIEF: MANSUTRENDZGH FREE WOOCOMMERCE POS INTEGRATION Prepared:
3 November 2026

ROLE You are an AI implementation agent working on the existing
MansutRENDZGH WordPress/WooCommerce website. Your task is to assess and,
only after approval, configure a free point-of-sale (POS) solution that
uses the existing WooCommerce catalogue and inventory.

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE Set up a physical-store POS workflow linked to
MansutRENDZGH.com so that in-store and online sales use the same
WooCommerce products and stock records.

CLIENT REQUIREMENTS - One physical retail outlet. - Existing POS
hardware is available. Do not assume compatibility; identify each device
and model first. - 1–3 staff members. - Payment methods required: Cash,
Mobile Money (MoMo), and Card. - Required functions: 1. Inventory
tracking. 2. Sales reports. 3. Barcode scanning. 4. Receipt printing. -
The existing website is MansutRENDZGH.com and uses WordPress +
WooCommerce.

PREFERRED STARTING OPTION Evaluate the free version of wePOS by weDevs:
https://wordpress.org/plugins/wepos/

Official product information: https://wedevs.com/wepos/ Check the
current plugin listing, documentation, compatibility, free-versus-paid
feature limits, and current release information before implementation.
Do not rely on old feature lists or assume a feature is included in the
free version.

IMPORTANT PAYMENT REQUIREMENT The client needs Cash, MoMo, and Card
payment recording. Distinguish clearly between: - Recording an
externally collected payment in the POS; and - Processing/collecting a
payment directly through an integrated payment gateway or terminal.

Do not claim that the free version supports direct Ghanaian MoMo or
card-terminal processing unless current official documentation and a
successful test confirm it. If the free version only supports cash or
basic/manual payment recording, document that limitation. Do not
purchase a paid extension or subscription, connect a live payment
gateway, or incur any charge without written approval.

NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY RULES 1. Do not make changes to the live website
until the audit is complete and the owner approves the implementation
plan. 2. Create and verify a full backup of website files and database
before making changes. 3. Prefer a staging site for installation and
testing. If staging is unavailable, stop and request approval before
testing on the live site. 4. Do not delete, overwrite, bulk-edit, or
import products, variations, orders, customers, stock quantities,
settings, or payment records. 5. Do not change the active theme,
redesign the website, alter SEO settings, change domain/DNS/email
settings, or modify unrelated plugins. 6. Do not disable security,
backup, cache, or payment plugins without approval and a documented
reason. 7. Do not expose administrator passwords, API keys, payment
credentials, customer data, or other secrets in logs or reports. 8. Do
not use real customer payments for tests. Use test mode or a clearly
identified test product/order where possible. 9. Do not buy licences,
extensions, hosting, hardware, or services. 10. If a requested function
is unavailable in the free version, report the gap and propose options.
Do not silently substitute a paid product.

PHASE 1 — DISCOVERY AND AUDIT (READ-ONLY) Inspect and report: -
WordPress version. - WooCommerce version and status. - PHP version and
hosting environment, where accessible. - Active theme and relevant child
theme. - Installed plugins that affect checkout, inventory, payments,
shipping, caching, security, backups, or product management. - Current
WooCommerce inventory settings, including stock management and
stock-hold settings. - Current payment gateways and whether Paystack is
installed/configured. - Number of products and product variations. -
Whether products use attributes such as size, colour, style, or other
variations. - Whether SKUs already exist and whether they are unique. -
Current stock-data quality and any products with missing or duplicate
SKUs. - Available backup and staging arrangements. - Exact hardware
models: POS computer/tablet, barcode scanner, receipt printer, cash
drawer, and any card terminal. - Whether the scanner behaves as a
keyboard input device and whether the printer supports the POS/browser
printing method.

Do not disclose customer personal information in the audit. Use counts
and configuration summaries only.

PHASE 2 — FREE SOLUTION VERIFICATION Before installation: 1. Check the
current official wePOS plugin page and documentation. 2. Confirm the
free version’s current support for: - WooCommerce product catalogue. -
Stock reduction after POS sales. - Stock updates visible to online
shoppers. - Variable products and variations. - Barcode/SKU search or
scanning. - Receipt printing and supported printer types. - Cash
payment. - Manual/custom payment methods, if available. - MoMo and card
payment recording. - Direct payment gateway or terminal integration. -
Sales reports and daily totals. - Multiple cashier accounts and
permissions. - Offline operation and synchronisation, if claimed. 3.
Separate features available in the free version from paid features. 4.
Record official documentation links and the date checked. 5. If a
required feature is paid-only or unclear, flag it. Do not assume it
works.

PHASE 3 — IMPLEMENTATION PLAN FOR APPROVAL Prepare a short
implementation plan containing: - Proposed plugin and exact version. -
Confirmed free features. - Requirements not met by the free version. -
Hardware compatibility findings. - Payment workflow for Cash, MoMo, and
Card. - Backup and rollback plan. - Staging test plan. - Any risks,
limitations, or costs.

STOP and request owner approval before installing or configuring
anything.

PHASE 4 — STAGING INSTALLATION (ONLY AFTER APPROVAL) On staging only: 1.
Confirm the backup can be restored or that a recent backup is available.
2. Install wePOS from the official WordPress plugin repository. 3.
Record plugin version and installation date. 4. Configure only the
settings needed for the single store. 5. Use the existing WooCommerce
catalogue. Do not create a second product database. 6. Confirm
WooCommerce stock management is enabled where appropriate. 7. Configure
cashier access with least privilege. Do not share the administrator
account with cashiers. 8. Configure payment labels only where supported.
Use clear labels such as Cash, Mobile Money, and Card for manual
recording if the plugin supports this. 9. Configure receipt details
using verified business information supplied by the owner. Do not invent
a legal business name, address, phone number, or tax number. 10. Connect
hardware only after confirming model compatibility and safe driver/setup
requirements.

PHASE 5 — PRODUCT AND BARCODE PREPARATION - Preserve all existing
product names, prices, images, categories, attributes, and variations. -
Do not bulk-generate or overwrite SKUs without approval. - Identify
products/variations without SKUs and provide a report. - Recommend a
consistent SKU pattern for approval before applying it. - For fashion
products, stock should be tracked separately by each size/colour
variation where the catalogue supports variations. - Test scanning
against a small number of approved products first. - Do not print or
attach labels in bulk until the owner approves the barcode/SKU mapping.

PHASE 6 — TESTING Use staging and test products/orders. Record evidence
for each test.

A. Product and inventory - Search for an existing simple product in the
POS. - Search for a variable product. - Scan a known barcode and confirm
the correct product/variation is selected. - Complete a test POS sale. -
Confirm the corresponding WooCommerce order is created. - Confirm stock
decreases by the correct quantity. - Confirm the updated stock quantity
appears on the online store. - Confirm that a cancelled order/refund
behaves as documented. - Confirm that an online test order updates the
stock seen by the POS.

B. Payments - Record a cash sale. - Test the MoMo workflow using a
test/manual record only. - Test the card workflow using a test/manual
record only. - Confirm payment method labels appear correctly in the
order and reports. - Do not process real payments. - Clearly document if
MoMo/card collection happens outside the POS and is only recorded inside
it.

C. Hardware - Test barcode scanner input. - Test receipt printing, paper
size, alignment, and readable text. - Test cash drawer operation only if
the hardware and POS support it. - Do not change device firmware or
install untrusted drivers.

D. Reports and staff - Confirm which sales totals and reports the free
version provides. - Confirm the report can distinguish payment methods
if supported. - Create/test cashier accounts only with owner approval. -
Confirm cashiers cannot access WordPress administration or sensitive
customer/payment settings unless explicitly authorised.

E. Connectivity - Test normal internet operation. - If offline POS is
advertised, test offline sales and synchronisation in staging. - Check
for duplicate orders, incorrect stock, or overselling after
reconnection. - Do not promise safe offline operation until this test
passes.

PHASE 7 — HANDOVER Provide: - Summary of what was installed and
configured. - Plugin name, version, and official documentation links. -
Features confirmed to work in the free version. - Features not available
or not verified. - Hardware models tested and results. - Payment
workflow and limitations. - Test results with pass/fail status. -
Instructions for opening the POS, searching/scanning products, recording
payments, printing receipts, and closing the day’s sales. - Staff access
details through the owner’s approved password-sharing method. Never
include passwords in the handover document. - Backup location/date and
rollback steps. - Recommended next steps and any optional costs
requiring owner approval.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA The project is complete only when: 1. POS sales
create WooCommerce orders. 2. POS sales reduce the correct WooCommerce
stock quantity. 3. Online sales update the stock available to the POS.
4. Product variations are handled correctly. 5. Barcode scanning works
with the client’s actual scanner. 6. Receipt printing works with the
client’s actual printer. 7. Cash, MoMo, and Card workflows are clearly
documented and tested to the extent possible without real payments. 8.
Available sales reports are demonstrated. 9. Staff access is
appropriately restricted. 10. The owner receives the handover report and
approves any live deployment.

FINAL INSTRUCTION Work in small, reversible steps. Begin with the
read-only audit and free-version feature verification. Return the
findings and implementation plan for approval. Do not install on the
live site, make purchases, alter stock, or process payments until
approval is explicitly given.
