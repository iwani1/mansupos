## Material release blockers / independent review questions

1. **Shared-stock proof is missing.** Inspect the actual installed Woo/WP/PHP/HPOS and InnoDB engines, theme/snippet/hook overrides and exact parent/variation stock owners **read-only** first. With separately authorized disposable local Woo and synthetic products, reproduce concurrent website/POS last-unit holds, native one-time payment reduction, cancellation/expiry, order metadata, tax/rounding and failure injection. The synthetic `ReserveStock` stub does **not** prove Woo/MySQL locking or external hook effects.
2. **Late website payment remains critical.** The draft does not intercept an installed Paystack callback that pays an expired website order or bypasses `payment_complete()`. Real gateway capture, callbacks, emails and fulfilment cannot be rolled back by SQLite. Until the actual path is audited and safely simulated, strict no-oversell and live sale approval remain **NO-GO**.
3. **Cash/expiry/restore need a policy and a proven protocol.** Local cash may be recorded before Woo sees a cash-intent marker. Conservative expiry deliberately leaves possible orphaned pending orders and holds for owner review rather than automatically cancelling them. Define monitored cleanup, TTL, cash return/liability, original vs paying shift, refunds/stock return, notification side effects and paired POS/Woo backup/restore handling. Do not infer a missing local row means a missing Woo order or unpaid customer.
4. **Wholesale and operations are incomplete.** Tax-enabled wholesale is explicitly locked; real tax-inclusive quote/receipt and adviser requirements, eligibility, physical stock count, staff roles, printer/drawer operation and business acceptance still require separate decisions and independent checks. The plugin custom capability is not auto-granted; an approved service account/installation design is outstanding.
5. **Phase 1/pilot/privacy remain independent gates.** Phase 1 restore/ledger changes need qualified independent disposition; the actual read-only pilot ledger cannot be assumed v9-bound or safe to upgrade. Production/off-host recovery, historical public-repository business data and private review access need owner decisions. None is repaired by a local build.
