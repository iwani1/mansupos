## F. Proposed cash-refund policy — **owner decision only; NOT implemented**

1. **Authorization and proof:** propose an owner/manager approval independent of the paying cashier, with a recorded reason, original POS sale/receipt and Woo order, actual Woo refund ID/status, original tender type, purchaser acknowledgment where appropriate, approver, payer, witness, timestamps and a signed/traceable payout receipt. Decide whether a second witness is mandatory and what minimum customer data may be retained. A Woo refund observation alone is not authorization or payout proof.
2. **Actual drawer attribution:** propose a dedicated payout record linked to both the original sale **and the shift that physically paid cash**, plus an immutable event trail. The original sale shift and any closed counted shift remain unchanged. A later cash payout reduces only the *currently open paying shift* after proof and approval; do not backdate a generic cash-out. Decide how to record a payout made outside an open shift and who resolves the resulting variance before any workflow is built.
3. **Amount controls:** design an idempotency key and unique original-sale/Woo-refund/payout linkage; authorize each partial payout separately; prohibit duplicate payouts and cumulative cash refunds above the original **cash actually collected** or Woo-approved refundable amount. Re-evaluate the remaining balance under transaction lock before every payout. Do not treat a card/MoMo refund, mixed tender, a changed/decreased remote refund, or a negative/over-refund as cash. Require owner review for mismatches.
4. **Goods and stock:** inspect returned goods and record condition/quantity and who checked them. A refund must **not** automatically put stock back: choose restock, quarantine, repair/write-off or no return under a separately approved Woo stock action, preventing double restock across website and till. Retain original and corrected receipt/invoice/tax evidence as required by a qualified adviser.
5. **Discrepancy workflow:** if Woo is refunded but cash has not been paid, show an unresolved liability, not a drawer deduction. If physical cash left but Woo/POS evidence is missing, freeze reconciliation, preserve the shift count, investigate with the owner, and resolve the discrepancy without rewriting history. Partial/repeated/changed Woo refunds must be reconciled by ID and amount, not inferred from status alone. Only after the owner approves this policy should a dedicated schema/API/UI and tests be designed; **none is implemented here**.

Current behavior is narrower: reconciliation reduces *net-sales reporting* for Woo refunds, records `refund.observed` and an unverified-cash warning, but **does not record a payout or paying shift**. An open drawer expects opening float + gross confirmed cash + recorded movements; a closed shift's snapshot cannot be edited/backdated. A previously paid sale under review retains recorded cash/report history; unresolved sales block shift close, but an observed refund by itself is not payout evidence and does not reduce expected cash. The generic movement button is **not** an approved refund workflow.

## G. Backup, restore, retention and post-restore procedure

- **Verified in isolation, including the later repair:** consistent SQLite online backup; manifest has format, timestamp, app version, mode, service identity, ledger UUID, schema, size, SHA-256, sale/pending/refund counts and FK result. Missing/tampered manifest, wrong mode/service/UUID, missing/partial v9 tables/columns/indexes/triggers, orphan references, v1/v2, and a held instance lease are refused. A full v8 staging image can be restored **locked**, recovered offline, and upgraded while retaining historical trade/buyer rows. A permitted same-ledger replacement preserves a separate manifest-verified safety copy including committed WAL data. Newer sale, refund observation or Woo-create-intent rows **and backup-only deleted users/receipts** refuse replacement. Sessions are cleared and an audit event records the action. Non-demo real accounts, including those from an empty-target restore, stay inactive with unusable passwords until explicit offline owner recovery. The selected owner alone is reset/re-enabled, must change the new temporary password at login, and may only later reset/authorize other staff individually; old credentials remain unusable.
- **Important limits:** the SHA manifest is not signed or encrypted; it does not protect against someone with write access to both files. The guard compares protected *local* rows, not remote Woo-only activity or actual money. A fresh empty target has no active ledger to compare; **the account quarantine prevents silent login resurrection but cannot prove that a deleted receipt or other business event did not exist after backup**. Reconcile with independently preserved POS/Woo/cash evidence before considering a fresh restore. Restored v3–v7 real images, power loss during swap and off-host recovery were not tested. A conservative refusal after later login/audit activity may require an owner-supervised offline incident procedure; do not add a force flag to bypass it casually. The safety copy is not permission to discard newer transactions. POS backup never substitutes for WordPress files/database backup or reverses Woo emails, stock, payments or fulfilment.
- **Proposed, not configured:** separate encrypted off-host, access-controlled and versioned copies for each pilot/trading POS ledger *and* WordPress; keep `.db` plus `.manifest.json` together, verify checksum after transfer, monitor backup freshness/capacity, perform periodic isolated restores with written evidence, maintain separate key custody and a documented retention schedule approved by the owner. The generic wrapper currently **does not prune** by default; `RETENTION_DAYS` deletes only explicitly aged manifested local pairs and must remain unset until off-host validation and retention approval. No off-host copy or key/retention policy was created here.
- **Before any later restore:** stop the exact service; confirm its unit, env, mode, service/UUID, backup provenance and manifest; compare active local and Woo ledgers/payments/cash/stock; preserve both images; rehearse with isolated **local** databases (no separate staging server); authorize the recovery window and **separate** offline owner reset; then re-check Woo order keys, paid states, refunds, pending orders and actual cash before reopening. A restore cannot be used to erase an uncertain sale or authorize a replacement order.

## H. Authentication, permissions, journals and shift accounting

The API authenticates bearer/cookie sessions, rejects conflicting credentials, rate-limits login, enforces password change/lockout/reset and role boundaries. Owner-only staff, audit and connection management; owner/manager recovery queue/events/reports/wholesale approval; cashier limited to own sales/shifts and denied those review endpoints.
