# Existing Mansu POS ↔ WooCommerce integration: implementation status

This is the **existing** React/Vite + Node/Express + SQLite Mansu POS, not a new POS or a replacement for WordPress/WooCommerce. WooCommerce remains the website, authoritative product catalogue, retail prices, variations and shared inventory. The POS uses server-side WooCommerce REST API access; no browser or public Woo response receives private POS wholesale defaults.

## Verified boundary

- Public Woo Store API read-only inspection of the owner-selected Mansu Shirt Top (Pink) shows a published variable GHS product with size choices. Owner-supplied screenshots give limited Woo admin stock-setting evidence below, but do **not** prove tax configuration, per-option available counts, order hooks or last-unit safety. Actual POS-vs-online order side effects have **not** been inspected or accepted here.
- The owner reports that the Contabo **1.2.2 read-only pilot** and matching-hash encrypted off-host pilot backup are in place; public HTTPS `/api/health` and `/api/ready` also reported `1.2.2`, `mode: live`, `liveReadOnly: true`. No trading service, live POS order or shared physical-source stock cutover is confirmed. The pilot stays locked and keeps its Read-only Woo key and ledger.
- The separate live-trading service template is **not installed**. It pins all checkout gates off in its systemd unit; an environment-file-only edit cannot permit sales. Branch 1.2.3 is a code change, **not deployed to Contabo**.

## Stock settings: read-only finding

The owner's WooCommerce **Products → Inventory** screenshot shows **global** stock management enabled and **Hold stock = 60 minutes**. Low/out-of-stock thresholds are 2/0; notification checkboxes, including backorder notifications, are enabled. A notification setting does **not** mean individual products allow backorders. This screen does not establish which product/variation manages the actual quantity, its backorder policy, a stock reservation, or the installed Woo version and plugin behavior. The public parent product's stock display is not a count for every size.

For **Mansu Shirt Top (Pink)** (Woo parent 6312), the owner then showed the product's **Product data → Inventory** tab: **Track stock quantity for this product** is checked, the configured parent quantity is **10**, **Do not allow backorders** is selected, the low-stock threshold inherits the store-wide **2**, and **Sold individually** is unticked. The editor explicitly says these settings apply to variations without manual stock management. The earlier screenshot of its **S** variation (6583) showed **Manage stock?** unticked. Thus, under the displayed Woo settings, **S inherits the parent's shared 10-unit stock pool and no-backorder policy**; it does *not* have 10 units reserved for S alone. The M/L variations have not been individually checked and may or may not manage their own quantities. This is a configured Woo balance, **not** a fresh physical recount or proof of actual REST/order stock behavior; installed plugins/filters may also affect it. No Woo settings were changed by this work. The existing POS maps parent-managed variations to a common stock key and aggregates cart quantities before creating/paying an order, but this precheck cannot serialize concurrent website and counter purchases.

In this POS source, Woo orders are created through the REST API as `created_via: mansu-pos` with `status: pending`. In the [WooCommerce core stock hooks inspected](https://github.com/woocommerce/woocommerce/blob/b2c3a50e6a8337cc2a20e160d3e453b97d7426af/plugins/woocommerce/includes/wc-stock-functions.php#L438-L465), checkout stock reservation is attached to `woocommerce_checkout_order_created`, not to arbitrary REST order creation. The [core unpaid-order cancellation default inspected](https://github.com/woocommerce/woocommerce/blob/b2c3a50e6a8337cc2a20e160d3e453b97d7426af/plugins/woocommerce/includes/wc-order-functions.php#L1138-L1206) includes orders created via `checkout` or `store-api`, **not `mansu-pos`**. The screenshot's 60-minute setting therefore cannot be treated as proof that this POS's pending orders reserve stock or auto-cancel. The actual installed version, custom filters, and plugins have not been verified; do not assume the store behaves exactly like this upstream source either.

This branch now refuses to treat an unexpected unpaid Woo status (`on-hold`, custom, etc.) or a `pending` order with a paid timestamp as payable after reconciliation; POS cancellation also requires a confirmed pending-to-cancelled response. These are **fail-closed status checks**, not a reservation, a fulfilment check, or a last-unit fix. The POS fresh stock/order reads and subsequent Woo writes are separate: a website checkout, admin action or plugin may change stock or order status between them. Current availability validation also honours Woo's *untracked* and permitted-backorder settings rather than requiring a finite stock pool for every item; those settings need an explicit owner-approved policy or a live fail-closed rule before claiming shared-stock correctness. Pending POS orders need explicit owner monitoring and safe resolution; never assume they expire in 60 minutes, collect cash on an uncertain order, or release goods before confirmed payment. See [LIVE-CASH-ROLLOUT.md](LIVE-CASH-ROLLOUT.md). The pilot remains read-only.

## Missing integration work (do not claim operational yet)

1. **POS → Woo product management:** design owner/inventory-manager forms to create/update Woo products, variations, images, categories, retail prices and draft/publish status in the **one Woo catalogue**, not a second POS stock table. Require unique reviewed SKU/product-ID crosswalk; many existing variation SKUs are blank. Preview changes and guard every Woo product write independently of cash checkout. Do not bulk-import the historical CSV or guess fuzzy matches.
2. **Physical-source cutover:** approve each identity/layout, website retail price, tax treatment and fresh physical count; quarantine the 41 held records. Draft/import/publish only after owner review and backup. The retired POS-only In-store cash/stock gate stays off.
3. **Shared stock correctness:** verify which exact products/options manage stock, whether backorders are allowed, and the installed store's REST-created `mansu-pos` pending/paid/cancel/refund behavior. Do not rely on the global 60-minute hold for POS reservations or auto-cancellation. Implement an effective Woo-side or owner-approved enforceable operational control for concurrent online vs counter **last-unit** sales; a POS read followed by a Woo write is not atomic. Require fulfilment-aware cancellation/restock and monitored resolution of stranded pending orders. Inventory adjustment history must come from audited Woo movements/approved counts, not a separate balance in SQLite; only physically received returns may be restocked.
4. **Consolidated reporting:** read Woo online sales with a separately approved credential in the distinct trading service, distinguish them from POS Retail/Wholesale by verified order metadata, reconcile refunds and payments, and implement channel totals/unit sales and stock-movement history. The read-only pilot must remain product-only and never read customer orders.
5. **Authorised staff:** design a limited inventory-manager role and an explicitly approved Wholesale cashier permission without granting full owner/manager controls. Until that is implemented, owner/manager alone can approve Wholesale quotes; cashiers cannot alter private prices.
6. **Operational acceptance:** review real tax/rounding, order notifications/integrations, off-host full WordPress and **separate trading-ledger** restore evidence, genuine supervised cash transactions (no staging clone was chosen), and hardware/recovery before permitting a first Woo order, then before opening to staff. Never enable writes on the pilot.

This roadmap is implementation work, **not** permission to install a Read/Write Woo key, change website stock or place a test order on the production store.
