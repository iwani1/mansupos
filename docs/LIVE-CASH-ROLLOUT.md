This Woo write plan does **not** authorize importing or selling them yet; verify identities, retail tax/prices, fresh counts and shared last-unit safeguards first.

The owner has **reported** written approval for real Retail and Wholesale sales and a full off-site WordPress backup with a tested restore. The operator chose not to use a separate test store. These reports do not establish tax, stock, payment-recovery or integration acceptance. Start with the [read-only settings and side-effect evidence worksheet](LIVE-READONLY-REVIEW.md); it does not authorize an order. The GreenGeeks WooCommerce shop continues to take online Paystack payments; the Contabo POS catalogue pilot (`pos.mansutrendzgh.com`) remains **read-only**, with its Read-only Woo key, service, port and ledger untouched. Do not turn it into a trading service or change its Woo key to Read/Write.

## Boundary for a later, separately reviewed trading deployment

- It needs its **own** service, private ledger/backups, local port and approved HTTPS routing; do not share the pilot's ledger, overwrite its Nginx virtual host or disturb the other VPS app. No trading unit or new Woo key is authorized by this plan.
- First scope is **cash only** for both Retail and Wholesale. The server rejects MoMo/card payment recording in `live` mode, even from a direct API call; the UI only offers cash. POS does **not** initiate or verify Paystack payments. Never record an attempted Paystack payment as cash. Online Paystack checkout stays available and unchanged.
- Preparing a POS order is a **real WooCommerce write** even before payment. WooCommerce, its plugins, webhooks, email and fulfilment tools can act on a pending order; backups cannot recall messages or deliveries. `set_paid` and the final Woo status can also trigger side effects. Do not use synthetic test orders or real customer money merely as a test.

**Stock timeout is not a POS safety gate.** The owner showed global stock management ON and a 60-minute Hold stock setting. For one example, owner screenshots show all three sizes S, M and L of Mansu Shirt Top (Pink) with individual stock management unticked, inheriting one parent-managed 10-unit pool with backorders off. That configured balance is not a fresh count or proof about the rest of the catalogue. This POS creates `pending` orders over Woo REST with `created_via: mansu-pos`; the [inspected Woo core hooks](INTEGRATION-STATUS.md#stock-settings-read-only-finding) reserve checkout orders and default to auto-cancelling customer-checkout unpaid orders, not these POS REST orders. Installed version/plugins/filters may differ, and none of this proves what happens on this store. Until the actual behavior and a shared last-unit control are accepted, monitor and resolve pending POS orders explicitly with the owner; never suppose that waiting 60 minutes returns stock or makes a new sale safe. Branch status checks reject an unexpected unpaid order or an unconfirmed cancellation response, but cannot make Woo payment and concurrent website checkout atomic.

## Gates to a supervised genuine cash sale (all must be recorded first)

1. **Read-only GreenGeeks check:** identify active Woo order webhooks, order emails, fulfilment/shipping/accounting/stock integrations and Paystack's trigger conditions. Decide who will receive *both* pending and paid POS-order notifications, and whether they will act on them. Do not disable store-wide services that online orders need. If side effects cannot be made safe for one controlled order, stop and arrange isolation instead.
2. Record the actual Woo tax-inclusive/exclusive settings, rate/class/location rules, delivery-versus-in-store policy, expected GHS totals and rounding for the chosen real goods. Read-only: identify the installed Woo/plugin versions and each chosen variation's Manage stock, stock quantity and backorder policy; review any relevant custom hold/cancellation filters. Agree how to monitor and safely cancel **unfulfilled** REST-created POS `pending` orders; the 60-minute global setting is not proof of reservation or expiry. Confirm real `pending`, paid and cancellation stock/email/fulfilment effects **only under approved acceptance**, not by inventing a test order. POS's fresh stock check and Woo write are **not atomic**; arrange an owner-approved, verifiable control for last-item/concurrent online sales. Do not assume the read-only catalogue verifies payable totals.
3. Verify the reported **full off-host WordPress files/database backup and restore evidence** (without restoring over live orders). Create and test an off-host backup/isolated restore for the **separate trading POS ledger**. Confirm emergency contacts, owner-controlled reconciliation, real receipt business details, shifts, drawer, scanner/printer and that the existing website and other VPS app still work.
4. The owner approves a written **controlled live-acceptance plan**: one real low-risk in-store cash sale, actual goods and customer/owner purchase, operator, timing, expected gross/tax/stock, notification recipients, failure owner and handling of a pending or uncertain order. A refund, deletion or database restore is **not** a substitute for handling real notifications or stock. Do not admit general POS users yet.
5. Only after steps 1–4 pass, an administrator can review a **separate** trading deployment and dedicated limited Woo credentials. Its initial checkout access should be limited to the supervised operator. Check the Woo order once and inspect tax/total before taking the exact cash; count cash before recording it; do not release goods until the Woo/POS order is confirmed Paid, stock and side effects are correct and a receipt is verified. On any mismatch or uncertain response, **stop** and reconcile that same order; do not retry with a fresh cart, mark paid manually or take another payment. Preserve ledger/order evidence. If an unpaid order must be cancelled, review the Woo side effects first.
6. Repeat under a separately approved Wholesale plan: owner/manager-only negotiated **all-in final unit** amounts (including any applicable tax), optional buyer name, actual goods and full cash before release. Check the actual Woo tax calculation for named and unnamed buyers, quantities, mixed tax classes and cent rounding; exact gross must equal the agreement. Keep Wholesale locked if not verified. Historical named-buyer orders remain readable; dispatch-on-credit is outside scope. Distinct live wholesale authorization and a reference to the documented live acceptance plan/record are required by the software; never set a *staging* approval flag to pretend staging passed.
7. Only after observed Retail and Wholesale results, payment/recovery drill, side effects, shared stock limits, printer/shift and backup evidence are signed off should access expand to staff. If a gate fails, keep or return the separate trading service to write-locked, keep the online shop open, and reconcile pending orders. Closing a write gate also blocks POS cancellation/payment; resolve unpaid orders with the owner in WooCommerce. Never restore an older ledger over newer sales.
