## H. Cash custody, unapplied-cash return and paid-sale refund — **owner policy proposals**

**Separate state facts:** `stock held`, `physical cash received`, `Woo confirmation pending/uncertain`, `Woo payment verified`, `sale complete/goods released`, `cash returned without a sale`, and `refund liability/physical payout` must not be collapsed into one `paid` boolean. Ideally cashier collects cash only after the hold and amount are verified; if cash has already physically entered the drawer while a Woo write is uncertain, record custody tied to actor/open shift/same attempt, block shift close and goods release, and reconcile. Do **not** call the unfinished Woo order a completed cash sale or collect a second tender on retry.

**Unapplied tender return (no paid Woo sale):** proposal: owner/manager approves a documented physical return of the *original unallocated cash* to the customer, separate from a Woo refund; record payer/witness/receipt, amount, original attempt and the **open shift that actually pays**; freeze old closed shift counts, prevent a duplicate return, keep unknown Woo status under review and do not auto-release a hold until cancellation is verified. Decide escalation if the customer leaves or a payment later appears. No API/table/payout workflow exists yet.

**Refund after a verified paid sale:** proposal: link Woo order **and exact refund ID/amount** to an owner-approved liability; verify returned goods/condition independently from payout and restock (default: no auto-restock), then record actual cash payer, witness/evidence and paying open shift once. A Woo refund alone changes observed net reporting **but is neither a drawer deduction nor payout proof**. If the original shift closed, retain its frozen count; debit only the later paying shift when cash really leaves, not by backdating. Duplicate/partial/changed refunds, external Woo refunds and missing cash evidence stay unresolved. The generic cash-out button is not an approved refund workflow. Owner must decide approval/witness, refund windows, proof/privacy/retention, installments, customer acknowledgement, tax/credit notes, restock authority, liability escalation and closed-/later-shift treatment **before implementation**.
